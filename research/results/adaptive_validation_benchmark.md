# Adaptive Validation Benchmark

This legacy artifact is retained for compatibility. The strengthened research evaluation is now in `baseline_comparison.md`, which adds uncertainty-only, cheapest-first, full-suite, and exact finite-set oracle baselines.

The original five-scenario measurements remain: mean modeled validation-cost reduction 63.5% and mean modeled uncertainty reduction 51.7%. These are deterministic engineering measurements, not statistical significance claims.
